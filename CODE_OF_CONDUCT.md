# Code of Conduct

This project is committed to a respectful, inclusive, and professional environment.
Participants should discuss on the basis of technical facts, respect different
backgrounds and experience, and avoid harassment, discrimination, personal attacks,
threats, and disclosing others' information without consent.

To report unacceptable behaviour, use the private channel described in the
[security policy](SECURITY.md). Maintainers may require corrections, remove
content, or restrict or ban participation.
